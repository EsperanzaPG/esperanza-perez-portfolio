About Me

I am a student of Industrial Design Engineering and Product Development at the University of Zaragoza.

I enjoy tackling challenges by integrating these two fields, aiming to create functional and thoughtful solutions.

I am also the eldest child in a large family, which has helped me develop a practical sense of creativity in everyday life. It has also taught me responsibility, adaptability, and the importance of teamwork from an early age.

I have participated in an Erasmus program in Porto, where I had the opportunity to collaborate with professionals and students from different disciplines and nationalities. This experience has given me a broader, more international perspective on the design field.

I am currently looking for internship opportunities in order to continue learning and developing my skills in a professional environment.
